Scam pop-ups are getting harder to spot, researchers warn

By Priya Natarajan · Nov 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Fake security warnings that mimic browser alerts are increasingly common on ad-supported sites, according to a report from a university security lab. The pop-ups often claim a device is infected and urge readers to “update” immediately.

Researchers recommend closing the tab rather than clicking any button in the pop-up. More background is in the full lab report and an earlier 2018 story on the same trend.

“The tell is pressure,” one author said. “Real software updates don’t count down a timer at you.”
